Keeping Your Cary Water Heater Running Longer

Many premature tank failures start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, have the anode rod inspected every few years, and test the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees for safer water and less scale. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard so the heat exchanger stays clean.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular service, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like discolored hot water, popping noises, or a damp spot under the tank, it's time to plan a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new heater with water spreading across the floor.

If warning signs do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A dripping relief valve might point to high water pressure or thermal expansion with nowhere to go, both inexpensive fixes. The same drip ignored for months can keep stressing the tank until a seam gives way, and a small repair becomes a full replacement plus water damage. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and cold showers.

Hot Water Headaches Cary Homeowners Face

In Cary, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with thermocouples and flame sensors, sooty burners, and draft problems in the flue. Electric units lose heating elements and thermostats, especially when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless heaters throw error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor trouble, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they struggle in cramped closets. We service all four types regularly and carry the parts to fix most problems on the first visit.
